# Eric Lundin / WannabeSamurai

Architect of **ERIC**: Execution, Reasoning, Integration, and Control.

I build private, local-first AI operating environments for work that should not depend on a public chatbot, a remote vendor's memory, or a pile of disconnected tools.

## ERIC

ERIC is not one model, one prompt, or one application. It is the system around the models: the part that keeps track of what is true, what the system can do, what it is allowed to do, what already happened, and what still needs to be checked.

Models are replaceable workers inside ERIC. ERIC is the structure that routes them, preserves operational state, controls tool use, verifies completed work, and keeps the same system available from PC/Web and Android.

## What This Means

> I install your own private AI for your business and maintain it.

Your company keeps its own data, its own hardware, and its own company-specific configuration. ERIC is installed and maintained as the underlying intelligence/control system—not rented as a shared public chatbot.

## What ERIC Is Built To Do

- Run locally with replaceable language models
- Keep durable conversations, tasks, evidence, and operational state
- Route work to models that have actually been qualified for it
- Control approved tools and devices through explicit, bounded authority
- Verify results instead of accepting a model's claim
- Recover interrupted work without blindly repeating an already-completed action
- Present the same authoritative system through PC/Web and Android clients
